FAQ

Quick answers.

What kind of videos do you edit?

Long-form gaming and Minecraft videos (pacing, zooms, sound design, multi-cam), viral YouTube Shorts / TikToks / Reels (kinetic captions, fast hook retention), talking-head creator intros, phonk/car speed-ramps, and After Effects motion graphics.

What software stack do you use?

Exclusively Adobe Premiere Pro for long-form narrative cutting, multi-cam pacing and sound mixing, and Adobe After Effects for kinetic typography, VFX compositing, speed ramping (RSMB, Sapphire, Deep Glow, BCC).

How fast are your turnarounds?

Shorts & Reels are typically delivered in 24 hours. Full long-form gaming edits take 24–48 hours depending on raw footage volume. Because of flexible working hours, rough cuts can be ready when you wake up.

What quality do you deliver in?

1080p 60fps standard up to 4K / 1440p 60fps master exports, color-corrected and optimized with faststart web streaming headers.

Can you match my channel's existing style?

Yes. Send 2–3 reference links or your previous uploads. I'll match your caption typography, sound palette, and pacing while upgrading viewer retention.

How do we get started?

Drop a message below or contact me on Discord / X / Email with a link to your raw footage (Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive). I can do a test cut or jump straight into your schedule.
